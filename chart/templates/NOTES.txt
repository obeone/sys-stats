The same data feeds the terminal client:

  sys-stats --url http://localhost:5000/stats

If a panel comes up empty:

  * Process tables need the host PID namespace (defaultPodOptions.hostPID,
    on by default) and enough privilege to read /proc.

  * GPU panels need a card claimed through the NVIDIA device plugin:

      --set controllers.main.containers.main.resources.limits.'nvidia\.com/gpu'=1

  * The Ollama panel stays empty until OLLAMA_API_URL points somewhere:

      --set controllers.main.containers.main.env.OLLAMA_API_URL=http://ollama:11434
